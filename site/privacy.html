Capyroom 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

Capyroom（以下称"本应用"）是一款专注计时应用，由北京天怡宝宝数字科技有限公司开发。本应用的设计原则是：无需登录即可使用计时功能；只有当你主动选择登录时，我们才会保存与账号相关的最少信息。

不登录时

不登录时，你的专注记录、编排、定时计划、设置和收藏保存在自己的设备上，不上传到账号同步服务器。下载场景素材等联网操作仍会产生下文所述的技术请求信息。

登录后（可选）

登录只用于把你的数据在多台设备之间同步、以及承载你买过的内容。你可以使用当前版本提供的登录方式（Google Play 版为 Google 登录，其他版本按实际提供 Apple 或手机号登录），我们会保存：

  • 账号标识：Apple 或 Google 提供的账号标识符（以及你授权提供的电子邮箱，仅用于显示"你登录的是哪个号"），或你输入的手机号。
  • 同步数据：专注记录、编排、定时计划、收藏与偏好设置。这些数据保存在我们位于中国境内的服务器上（海外版本保存在境外服务器上），仅用于在你的设备之间同步，不用于广告或画像，不出售给第三方；为登录、支付等服务所需的数据处理见下文。

手机号登录时，验证码通过第三方短信服务（腾讯云）发送，你的手机号仅为发送验证码而传递给该服务商。

你可以随时在"调汤 → 账号"里退出登录，或删除账号——删除后服务器上与该账号相关的账号资料、登录会话、同步记录和服务端购买权益会从在用账号数据库中删除；这台设备上的记录不受影响。

网络访问

本应用会从我们的服务器下载场景素材（动画视频、图片），该请求不携带任何个人标识。

内购与支付

  • App Store / Google Play：付费内容通过 Apple 的 App 内购买或 Google Play 结算完成，交易由 Apple / Google 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息。购买记录保存在你的设备上（登录后也会随账号同步）；"恢复购买"通过你的 Apple ID / Google 账号完成，与是否登录无关。

通知

若你允许通知，本应用只在本地排定计时提醒，不使用推送服务器。

技术请求信息与安全保护

网站和素材/API 服务会记录请求 IP 地址、请求时间、路径、响应状态及浏览器或客户端标识（User-Agent），用于运行服务、排查故障和防范滥用；不用于广告或跨应用跟踪。网络传输使用 HTTPS。账号接口需要验证登录凭据，账号同步请求需携带有效会话凭据。服务端数据限授权人员为提供和维护服务而访问。

数据保留与删除

本地记录由你管理；退出登录不会自动删除本地记录或云端账号。账号资料与同步记录在账号存续期间保留，直至你删除相关记录或申请删除账号。登录会话在连续 400 天未使用后过期并由定期清理处理；这不等于删除账号。网站访问日志按天轮换，保留最近 14 份轮换日志。

你可以在“调汤 → 账号”中删除账号，也可以通过下方邮箱申请删除账号：注明 Capyroom、登录方式以及可用于确认账号归属的信息，我们会核验并处理。请勿发送密码、验证码或银行卡信息。删除不会取消 Google Play 或 App Store 保存的交易记录，平台按各自规则处理这些记录；平台购买仍可使用恢复购买。删除范围为我们在用服务中的账号数据；已与账号解除关联的技术日志按日志轮换清理，不承诺所有技术记录即时清除。

适用版本与服务提供方

Google Play 版的身份登录和购买由 Google 提供；App Store 版适用 Apple 提供的对应服务。平台会按其隐私政策处理你直接提供给它的数据。手机号登录仅适用于提供该入口的版本。上述服务商只在你使用相关功能时参与处理。国内官网安卓版的支付及账号补充说明单独提供，不适用于 Google Play 版。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

Capyroom Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

Capyroom (“the app”) is a focus timer made by Beijing Tianyi Baobao Digital Technology Co., Ltd. The app is built on one principle: timer features work without signing in; only when you choose to sign in do we keep the minimum information needed for your account.

Without signing in

Without signing in, your sessions, plans, schedules, settings and collections stay on your device and are not uploaded to our account-sync service. Network operations such as downloading scene assets still generate the technical request information described below.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in exists only to sync your data across devices and to hold your purchases. You can use the sign-in methods offered in your edition (Google sign-in in the Google Play edition; Apple or phone sign-in where offered in other editions), and we store:

  • Account identifier: the identifier provided by Apple or Google (plus the email address if you choose to share it, shown only so you can see which account you used), or the phone number you entered.
  • Synced data: sessions, plans, schedules, collections and preferences. They are stored on our servers (in mainland China for the China edition, abroad for the international edition), used solely to sync between your devices, never for advertising or profiling, and not sold to third parties; necessary processing for sign-in and payments is described below.

For phone sign-in, the verification code is delivered by a third-party SMS provider (Tencent Cloud); your phone number is passed to that provider only to send the code.

You can sign out or delete your account at any time under Tune → Account. Deleting the account removes your account profile, sign-in sessions, synced records and server-side purchase entitlements from the active account database; the records on this device are not affected.

Network access

The app downloads scene assets (animation clips and images) from our server when needed. These requests carry no personal identifier.

In-app purchases and payments

  • App Store / Google Play: paid content is sold through Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing. Apple / Google processes the transaction; we never see your payment details. Purchase records are stored on your device (and synced with your account if you sign in). Restore Purchases works through your Apple ID / Google account regardless of sign-in.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules timer reminders locally. No push server is involved.

Technical requests and data security

Our website and asset/API services log the requesting IP address, request time, path, response status and browser or client identifier (User-Agent) to operate the service, diagnose failures and prevent abuse, not for advertising or cross-app tracking. Network communication uses HTTPS. Account endpoints verify sign-in credentials, and account-sync requests require a valid session credential. Access to server-side data is limited to authorized personnel providing and maintaining the service.

Retention and deletion

You control your local records. Signing out does not automatically delete local records or your cloud account. Account information and synced records are retained while your account exists, until you delete the relevant records or request account deletion. Sign-in sessions expire after 400 consecutive days without use and are periodically cleaned up; this does not delete your account. Website access logs rotate daily, with the latest 14 rotated logs retained.

You can delete your account under Tune → Account, or request deletion by emailing the contact below with “Capyroom”, your sign-in method and information that lets us verify account ownership. We will verify and process your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes or bank-card details. Deleting your account does not cancel transaction records held by Google Play or the App Store; those platforms handle them under their own rules, and platform purchases can still be restored. Deletion removes account data from our active service; technical logs no longer linked to an account are cleared through log rotation, rather than a promise that every technical record disappears immediately.

Editions and service providers

Google provides sign-in and purchases in the Google Play edition. Apple provides the corresponding services in the App Store edition. These platforms handle data you give them under their own privacy policies. Phone sign-in applies only to editions offering it. Providers participate when you use the relevant feature. Additional payment and account information for the Android edition distributed from our website in China is provided separately and does not apply to the Google Play edition.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
