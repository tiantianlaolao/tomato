Capyroom 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日

Capyroom（以下称"本应用"）是一款专注计时应用，由北京天怡宝宝数字科技有限公司开发。本应用的设计原则是：不登录也能使用全部功能；只有当你主动选择登录时，我们才会保存与账号相关的最少信息。

不登录时

本应用不收集、不上传任何个人信息或使用数据。你的专注记录、编排、定时计划、设置和收藏全部保存在你自己的设备上。

登录后（可选）

登录只用于把你的数据在多台设备之间同步、以及承载你买过的内容。你可以用 Apple、Google 或手机号登录，我们会保存：

  • 账号标识：Apple 或 Google 提供的账号标识符（以及你授权提供的电子邮箱，仅用于显示"你登录的是哪个号"），或你输入的手机号。
  • 同步数据：专注记录、编排、定时计划、收藏与偏好设置。这些数据保存在我们位于中国境内的服务器上（海外版本保存在境外服务器上），仅用于在你的设备之间同步，不用于广告或画像，不与第三方共享。
  • 购买记录（仅通过支付宝购买时）：订单号、商品、金额、支付宝交易号和付款时间，用于给你的账号开通已购内容、换机找回和对账；不含你的支付宝账号或银行卡信息。

手机号登录时，验证码通过第三方短信服务（腾讯云）发送，你的手机号仅为发送验证码而传递给该服务商。

你可以随时在"调汤 → 账号"里退出登录，或删除账号——删除后服务器上与该账号相关的全部数据（包括购买记录）会立即清除；这台设备上的记录不受影响。

网络访问

本应用会从我们的服务器下载场景素材（动画视频、图片），该请求不携带任何个人标识。

内购与支付

  • App Store / Google Play：付费内容通过 Apple 的 App 内购买或 Google Play 结算完成，交易由 Apple / Google 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息。购买记录保存在你的设备上（登录后也会随账号同步）；"恢复购买"通过你的 Apple ID / Google 账号完成，与是否登录无关。
  • 支付宝（中国版安卓官网直装包）：付款在支付宝完成，我们只收到支付宝返回的订单结果（是否付款、金额、交易号），不接触你的支付宝账号与银行卡信息。购买须登录，权益记在你的账号上。

通知

若你允许通知，本应用只在本地排定计时提醒，不使用推送服务器。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息。

联系

如有疑问，请写信至 support@tybbtech.com。

Capyroom Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026

Capyroom (“the app”) is a focus timer made by Beijing Tianyi Baobao Digital Technology Co., Ltd. The app is built on one principle: everything works without signing in; only when you choose to sign in do we keep the minimum information needed for your account.

Without signing in

The app collects and uploads nothing. Your sessions, plans, schedules, settings and collections stay on your device.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in exists only to sync your data across devices and to hold your purchases. You can sign in with Apple, Google or a phone number, and we store:

  • Account identifier: the identifier provided by Apple or Google (plus the email address if you choose to share it, shown only so you can see which account you used), or the phone number you entered.
  • Synced data: sessions, plans, schedules, collections and preferences. They are stored on our servers (in mainland China for the China edition, abroad for the international edition), used solely to sync between your devices, never for advertising or profiling, and never shared with third parties.
  • Purchase records (Alipay purchases only): order number, product, amount, Alipay transaction number and payment time, used to grant the purchase to your account, recover it on a new device and reconcile payments. No Alipay account or card details.

For phone sign-in, the verification code is delivered by a third-party SMS provider (Tencent Cloud); your phone number is passed to that provider only to send the code.

You can sign out or delete your account at any time under Tune → Account. Deleting the account immediately erases all data associated with it on our servers, including purchase records; the records on this device are not affected.

Network access

The app downloads scene assets (animation clips and images) from our server when needed. These requests carry no personal identifier.

In-app purchases and payments

  • App Store / Google Play: paid content is sold through Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing. Apple / Google processes the transaction; we never see your payment details. Purchase records are stored on your device (and synced with your account if you sign in). Restore Purchases works through your Apple ID / Google account regardless of sign-in.
  • Alipay (Android edition distributed from our website in China): payment is completed in Alipay; we receive only the order result Alipay returns (paid or not, amount, transaction number) and never touch your Alipay account or card details. Purchasing requires sign-in; the entitlement is recorded to your account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules timer reminders locally. No push server is involved.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
