Delete your Capyroom account and data

You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled Capyroom. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

Request account deletion by email

If the button does not open an email app, send your request from any email service to:
service@tybbtech.com

This button opens an email draft. Your request is sent when you send the email; opening this page or the draft does not delete your account.

What to include

  1. Use the subject Capyroom account deletion request.
  2. Tell us how you signed in. For the Google Play edition, this is Google sign-in. Include the email used for that account. For other editions, provide the Apple sign-in email (including an Apple private relay address, if used), or the phone number if you used phone sign-in.
  3. Say that you want your Capyroom account and associated data deleted. If possible, send the request from your sign-in email.

We will reply to verify account ownership and keep you informed of the request. If further information is needed, we will explain what is needed. After ownership is confirmed, we will process the deletion and email the result. Do not send passwords, verification codes, ID documents or bank-card information.

What is deleted

  • Your Capyroom account profile and sign-in association.
  • Sign-in sessions and device associations held by our account service.
  • Synced focus records, plans, schedules, preferences and collections.
  • Purchase and entitlement records associated with your account in our active account database.

What is not deleted by this request

  • Records stored only on your device. You control local data on that device.
  • Your Google or Apple account, or transaction records held by Google Play or the App Store. Those platforms handle them under their own policies. Deleting your Capyroom account does not request a refund; platform purchases can still be restored through the original store account.
  • Technical access logs are cleared through daily rotation, retaining the latest 14 rotated logs. Account deletion removes account data from our active service; it does not promise immediate erasure of every technical log entry.

Alternative: delete in the app

If you still have Capyroom, open Tune / Settings → Account → Delete account. This option is available after signing in. Email requests remain available if you cannot access the app.

删除 Capyroom 账号与相关数据

即使已经卸载 Capyroom，也可以在这里申请删除账号。提出申请无需重新安装 App。

通过邮件申请删除账号

按钮会打开邮件草稿，你可以用中文填写。如果无法打开邮件 App，请使用任意邮箱发送申请至：
service@tybbtech.com

邮件发送后才提出申请；仅打开页面或邮件草稿不会删除账号。

申请时请提供

  1. 邮件标题：Capyroom 账号删除申请。
  2. 登录方式与登录账号：Google Play 版为 Google 登录，请提供对应邮箱。其他版本按实际使用的 Apple 登录邮箱（包括 Apple 隐藏邮箱地址）或手机号填写。
  3. 说明需要删除 Capyroom 账号及相关数据；如可以，请从登录邮箱发来申请。

我们会通过邮件核验账号归属并告知申请进度。如需补充信息，会说明具体要求；核验完成后处理删除，并通过邮件告知结果。请勿发送密码、验证码、身份证件或银行卡信息。

删除范围

  • Capyroom 账号资料及登录关联。
  • 账号服务保存的登录会话和设备关联。
  • 已同步的专注记录、编排、定时计划、偏好设置与收藏。
  • 在用账号数据库中与该账号关联的购买记录和权益。

本次申请不删除的内容

  • 仅保存在设备上的本地记录，由你在该设备上管理。
  • 你的 Google 或 Apple 账号，以及 Google Play / App Store 保存的交易记录，由对应平台按其规则处理。删除 Capyroom 账号不等于申请退款；平台购买仍可通过原商店账号恢复。
  • 技术访问日志按天轮换，保留最近 14 份轮换日志。账号删除清除在用服务中的账号数据，不承诺所有技术日志即时消失。

也可以在 App 内直接删除

如果仍安装着 Capyroom，可在登录后打开调汤 / 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号。无法使用 App 时，仍可通过本页的邮件入口申请。
